# Pete's Enterprise Archetype Library

## Leadership Archetypes

### The Resume Padder
- Mandates AI initiative with aggressive timeline
- Leaves company before implementation completes
- Lists "AI Transformation Leader" on LinkedIn 3 weeks later
- **Signature move:** "We need to move fast" (translation: before my departure date)
- **Tell:** Already interviewing when they announce the initiative

### The Governance Vampire
- Creates 47-step approval process for any AI project
- Has never approved anything in recorded history
- Considers "risk mitigation" their entire job description
- **Signature move:** "We just need one more review cycle"
- **Tell:** Calendar is 100% meetings about meetings

### The Metrics Illusionist
- Redefines success criteria mid-project when original targets look unachievable
- Claims victory on technicality nobody agreed to
- Presents red dashboards as "opportunities for improvement"
- **Signature move:** "We exceeded our revised targets"
- **Tell:** Slides have more footnotes than data points

### The Strategy Deck Artist
- Produces beautiful PowerPoints that never become reality
- Confuses planning with execution
- Has strong opinions about AI but has never deployed anything
- **Signature move:** "Let me share my vision" (translation: 47 slides incoming)
- **Tell:** Says "strategic" more than any other word

## Vendor Archetypes

### The Demo Magician
- Uses cherry-picked data that doesn't resemble your actual environment
- Production accuracy mysteriously 40% lower than demo
- Blames your data quality when things fail
- **Signature move:** "That's a training data issue on your side"
- **Tell:** Demo environment has suspiciously clean data

### The Vaporware Evangelist
- Sells features on the roadmap as if they exist today
- "General availability" is always 2 quarters away
- References case studies that are actually POCs
- **Signature move:** "Our next release will address that"
- **Tell:** No reference customers will take your call

### The Implementation Partner Grifter
- Quotes 12 weeks, delivers in 18 months
- Junior consultants doing senior work
- Change orders exceed original contract value
- **Signature move:** "That's out of scope" (for things clearly in scope)
- **Tell:** Project manager changes every 6 weeks

### The AI Hype Surfer
- Learned "AI" in 2023, now an expert
- Drops "LLM" and "RAG" into every sentence
- Actual technical depth: can spell GPT
- **Signature move:** "We're leveraging generative AI" (translation: we added ChatGPT API)
- **Tell:** Can't explain how any of it actually works

## Internal Blocker Archetypes

### The Security Theater Director
- Review process designed to kill projects without saying no
- Asks for documentation that doesn't exist anywhere
- 6-month timeline for any approval
- **Signature move:** "We need to assess the risk profile"
- **Tell:** Has never approved a cloud service

### The Center of Excellence Bureaucrat
- Runs "AI CoE" that produces nothing but governance documents
- Headcount justification disguised as innovation leadership
- All initiatives require their blessing, none receive it
- **Signature move:** "That needs to go through our intake process"
- **Tell:** Team of 12, zero production deployments

### The Steering Committee Seat Warmer
- Attends monthly meetings, contributes nothing
- Asks same question every meeting
- Votes with whoever spoke last
- **Signature move:** "Can you remind me of the business case?"
- **Tell:** Has never read the pre-read materials

### The Passive-Aggressive Stakeholder
- Says "interesting" when they mean "no"
- Kills projects through inaction rather than opposition
- Never explicitly blocks, just never approves
- **Signature move:** "Let me socialize this with my team"
- **Tell:** Emails go unanswered for 2+ weeks

- Believes agents are variables in an equation
- **Signature move:** "The model says we need 47.3 agents"
- **Tell:** Has never taken a customer call

- Evaluates calls on 47-point rubric nobody designed for humans
- Marks agents down for not saying scripted phrase verbatim

- **Signature move:** "That's not how we trained you"
